Message content retention

PahlaviBot does not permanently store ordinary message bodies. Message content may be read temporarily while Discord events are being processed.

This processing may occur for counting, profanity detection, keyword-based reactions, ghost-ping-related behavior, or similar message-triggered functionality.

Although ordinary message content is not retained as a permanent message archive, derived information can be stored. For example, a profanity detector may retain a user's cumulative swear count, while the counting system may retain counting state or timestamps.

Command arguments are different

Slash-command names and their arguments/options may be written to command activity logs. Do not place passwords, authentication tokens, payment details, or similarly sensitive information into bot command arguments.

Privacy and data use

PahlaviBot uses stored information primarily to provide the features requested or configured by users and server administrators, maintain persistent server state, preserve economic records, generate statistics, and operate the service.

Data is not intentionally collected simply for the purpose of building a permanent archive of Discord conversations. Ordinary message bodies are processed only when needed by message-triggered features and are not treated as a permanent chat history.

Server administrators

Server administrators are responsible for determining which bot features are appropriate for their community and for configuring permissions correctly. Administrators should consider their own community's expectations and applicable privacy requirements before enabling features that collect persistent information.

Third-party infrastructure

PahlaviBot may use external infrastructure or services for hosting, backups, synchronization, networking, analytics, or other operational purposes. The exact providers used by the project may change during development.

PahlaviBot may also synchronize selected persistent data with backup infrastructure where necessary to protect against data loss and maintain service continuity.

Economy and virtual currency

PahlaviBot's economy is a fictional, server-specific system intended for entertainment, gameplay, simulation, and roleplay. Currency represented inside PahlaviBot has no inherent cash value and is not intended to represent real-world money.

Virtual currency

PahlaviBot does not currently provide a system for purchasing its fictional currency with real money. The currency cannot be treated as a bank deposit, investment, security, payment method, or claim against the bot's operator.

Economic functionality

  • Users may hold funds in wallets and private bank accounts.
  • Users may deposit and withdraw funds.
  • Users may receive government payments and payroll.
  • Users may purchase configured personal items.
  • Users may participate in supported resource trading.
  • Servers may operate treasury and national economic systems.

Administrative controls

Authorized administrators may have tools capable of creating or removing money, issuing government payments, changing economic parameters, modifying bank interest, or otherwise maintaining the server economy.

Such controls exist because PahlaviBot's economic systems are designed partly around simulated governments and server-specific economies. They should not be interpreted as authorization to manipulate another server or user's data without appropriate permission.

Economy exploits

If a bug or unintended behavior causes fraudulent money creation, the affected economy may be corrected. This may include removing duplicated currency, reversing transactions, disabling an affected command, restoring data from a verified backup, or otherwise repairing the affected economic state.

Intentionally exploiting an economic vulnerability may result in access restrictions or other action under these terms.

08

Message handling and moderation

PahlaviBot provides limited automated message functionality. It is not intended to replace a complete moderation system or the judgment of human server administrators.

Automated message actions

  • Invalid counting messages may be deleted.
  • Incorrect counting messages may be deleted.
  • Consecutive counting attempts by the same user may be deleted when prohibited by the counting system.
  • Configured swear words may be detected and associated with a user's cumulative swear count.
  • Certain keywords or phrases may trigger configured reactions or temporary responses.

What PahlaviBot does not currently do

  • Automatically ban users.
  • Automatically kick users.
  • Automatically timeout users.
  • Issue formal moderation warnings.
  • Maintain a general-purpose punishment database.
  • Override Discord server moderation decisions.

Discord permissions and the configuration chosen by the server's administrators remain authoritative.
